✦ Home-screen widget ✦

Trish Today

What you're shooting and posting today and tomorrow, from both apps.

What it shows
💅 / 🌸 Trish Badd shoot days · 📌 Pinterest pins · 📚 Titles by Trish videos, with the time and where to post. It refreshes about every 30 minutes, and tapping it opens your app.

1. Get Scriptable

Install Scriptable (free) from the App Store, then open it once.

2. Copy the widget

Loading…

3. Paste it into Scriptable

  1. In Scriptable, tap + (top right).
  2. Tap the empty page, then Paste.
  3. Tap the title at the top and name it Trish Today.
  4. Tap Done. Tap the script once to preview it.

4. Add it to your home screen

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) → search Scriptable.
  3. Pick the large size (today + tomorrow) or medium (today), then Add Widget.
  4. Tap the new widget while it's still jiggling → Script → choose Trish Today.
  5. Optional: in Parameter type badd for Trish Badd only or books for Titles by Trish only. Leave it empty for both. You can add two widgets, one of each.

Good to know

• Titles by Trish shares its calendar with the widget each time you open it. If book videos look out of date, open Titles by Trish once.

• Trish Badd shows the moment your phone syncs, so changes in the app appear on the next widget refresh.

• Offline? It shows the last update it saw.
